Privacy Policy

How NiksaMart collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: June 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to NiksaMart ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and protect your data when you use the NiksaMart platform.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Shipping Data: Delivery address, pincode, city, and state.
  • Profile Data: Avatar, preferences, and saved addresses.

B. Usage Data

  • Order History: Products purchased, order status, and transaction details.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for authentication and analytics to improve your experience.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To communicate order updates and customer support.
  • To personalize product recommendations.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Vendors: To fulfill orders (name, address, phone).
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay for secure payment processing.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard SSL encryption, secure password hashing (bcrypt), and regular security audits to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage your account settings or contact us at care@niksamart.com.
