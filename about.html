Our Journey Back to Roots

NiksaMart began with a simple yet profound realization: the modern world has lost touch with the purity of traditional foods. Commercial processing and adulteration have stripped our daily nutrition of its core vitality.

We embarked on a journey across rural India to rediscover the authentic Vedic methods of food preparation. From the sacred Bilona churning of A2 cow milk to the careful cold-pressing of oilseeds, NiksaMart is a celebration of heritage, health, and holistic well-being.
